How it works

  1. 1

    Step 1: Track a project

    Pick a project, pick or type a task, press Start. Your last three project and task pairs are one tap away. Switching closes the current entry and opens the next.

  2. 2

    Step 2: It stops itself when you go idle

    After 2 minutes with no keyboard or mouse input (you can set 1 to 15), the entry ends at your last activity. Time away is never added.

  3. 3

    Step 3: You choose what happens next

    Ask me shows how long you were gone, and Enter resumes. Auto-resume restarts the same task and tells you. Away longer than your limit, 2 hours by default, and it asks anyway.

Questions

What counts as idle?+

No keyboard or mouse input on your computer for your idle threshold, 2 minutes by default and adjustable from 1 to 15. Locking your screen counts too. The entry ends at your last activity, so the threshold itself is not tracked either.

Does it work during calls?+

If you listen for a while without touching the keyboard or mouse, it will treat that as idle and stop. For long calls, raise the idle threshold, or switch to Auto-resume so it picks up as soon as you move. The gap shows on your timeline, so you can add it back as a manual entry if it was billable.

How do I export my data?+

Open the dashboard and choose Export CSV or Export JSON. The file is created in your browser and saved to your downloads. Nothing is uploaded.

What happens after 6 months?+

Entries older than 6 months are deleted automatically from your device. Your projects and settings stay. If you need a longer history, export before then.

Can my team use it?+

Each person can install it for themselves, but there is no shared workspace, sync or team reporting, because there are no accounts or servers. People can export CSV files and combine them.
