Privacy policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

The short version: your time data never leaves your device. Wherewent has no account, no servers, no analytics, and the extension makes no network calls.

What the extension stores

  • The projects and tasks you create, with their names and colors.
  • Time entries: which project and task, and when each one started and ended.
  • Periods when you were away, so the dashboard can show them as gaps.
  • Your settings, such as the idle threshold and what happens when you return.

Where it is stored

In your Chrome profile's local extension storage, on the device you are using. It is not synced to other devices and it is not copied anywhere else. Uninstalling the extension removes this data from Chrome.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account to create, and the extension does not send your data, usage statistics, crash reports or identifiers to us or to anyone else. It contains no analytics or advertising code.

Idle detection

To stop the timer when you step away, the extension uses Chrome's built-in idle detection. It runs on your computer and only reports whether you are active, idle or locked. It does not record what you type, which sites you visit or what is on your screen.

How long data is kept

Entries older than 6 months are deleted automatically, on your device. You can delete individual entries at any time from the dashboard, or remove everything by uninstalling the extension.

Exporting

You can export your data as CSV or JSON from the dashboard. The file is created in your browser and saved to your downloads folder. What you do with it after that is up to you.

This website

This site is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies and loads no analytics, fonts or scripts from other companies. Fonts are served from this site. If you switch the theme, that choice is saved in your browser's local storage and never sent anywhere. The company hosting these files may keep standard request logs, such as IP address and browser type, as part of running the service.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it. Anything that would send data off your device would be a different product, and we would say so plainly.
